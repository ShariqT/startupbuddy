# StartupBuddy

Welcome to Statup Buddy! StartupBuddy is here to help you do hone your business idea, create a pitch for you, and a landing page that you drop into a coding agent to create a website. 

## What to do

To correctly use StartupBuddy, you need to have an idea. This idea is something that you 
have been thinking about in the back of head. The idea that makes you wonder, "why hasn't 
someone done this already?"

Once you have that idea, go to **Business Idea**. StatupBuddy will help you hone your business idea -- asking you the neccessary questions to fine-tine to this idea to pitch. 

After you have answered all of StartupBuddy's questions, you can go to **Create Your Pitch**. This where StartupBuddy will create a 10-minute pitch, an elevator pitch, and an email pitch that you can use for investors. 

The last step is to create a landing page for your business idea. This is done in **Website Mockup**. You can download the image that StartupBuddy generates and give that to any LLM -- Claude, Codex, etc and they will build out the website for you. The purpose of these landing page sites are to guage interest in your idea. Usually, people collect email addresses because this group will most likely be your first customers.
